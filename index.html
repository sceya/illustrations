Below you will find more details about the history of illustration and, in particular, about sceya — how it started, what it means, and where it is going.

Each piece is built around a single core thread: the idea that an illustration is not just an image, but a way to earn a living and, at the same time, the meaning of life itself.

From organic forms and perceptual interfaces to digital and biological layers — this is the territory where sceya operates.
