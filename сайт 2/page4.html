STAGE 01 / ROUGHSKETCH
STEP 01 / 05
From black dirt and lines, a fairly satisfying result can come — all it takes is a little effort and creativity.
从黑色的泥泞和线条中，可以得到相当令人满意的结果，只需付出一点努力和创意。
First, lay down the sketch as messily as possible. Pull general shapes straight from your head onto the canvas.
先把草图画得尽量凌乱。把脑海中的大致形象直接放到画布上。
Then mark which details to keep and which to drop. Finish the clean line sketch.
然后标出哪些细节要保留，哪些要舍弃。完成干净的线稿。
Next, work from a spot of colour or tone. Pick your colours and limits. Go.
接着从一块颜色或色调入手。选好颜色和限制。开始吧。
Finally, add the render. I believe in you.
最后，加上渲染。我相信你。
